⚠️ Common Issue: "Customer not found"

If you see "Customer not found" when viewing customer details, this means no sales records exist for that phone number. The system requires sales with the customerPhone field populated. Create a sale with customer information to see it appear in the customer list.

System Overview

A complete customer management system with dashboard, search, detailed profiles, and transaction history tracking.

📊 Dashboard
4 summary cards showing total customers, customers with due, outstanding balance, and new customers this month
🔍 Search & Filter
Advanced search by name/phone, status filtering (active/inactive), server-side pagination
👤 Customer Profiles
Complete financial history, sales records, collection timeline, and detailed analytics
📄 Statements
Printable customer statements with date range filtering and branded header

Access the System

Navigate to the customer management page:

URL: /admin/customers
Role Required: admin or superAdmin
Quick Start
  1. Go to /admin/customers in your app
  2. View the dashboard tab for summary metrics
  3. Switch to "All Customers" tab to see the customer list
  4. Click any customer to view their complete profile and history

API Endpoints

Method Endpoint Purpose
GET /api/admin/customers/dashboard Dashboard summary with 4 metrics
GET /api/admin/customers Customer list with search, filter, pagination
GET /api/admin/customers/[phone] Complete customer detail by phone number
GET /api/admin/customers/statement Customer statement with date range

Key Features

  • Customer Grouping: Automatically groups sales by customer phone number
  • Real-time Calculations: Total purchase, paid amount, and outstanding balance computed live
  • Activity Timeline: Complete history of sales, invoices, and collections
  • Search & Filter: Find customers by name, phone, or invoice number
  • Status Tracking: Active (last 90 days) vs inactive customers
  • Bangladesh Timezone: All dates in UTC+06:00 (Asia/Dhaka)
  • Role-based Access: Admin and superAdmin only
  • Printable Statements: Professional customer statements with Khalil Computer branding
